APPLICATION INFORMATION

About ZoneSync

A practical workspace for exploring local times and planning across timezones.

World clocks and planning

Explore timezones on the map, save world clocks, and convert dates between regions. Compare a selected location with up to five other timezones to find a suitable shared time.

Time accuracy

ZoneSync uses your device clock and your browser’s timezone database. Conversions follow the rules for the selected region and date, including daylight saving changes. Keep your device time and browser up to date.

Privacy and data

Saved clocks and appearance preferences stay in this browser. City searches are sent to Open-Meteo; maps, fonts, and libraries load from external providers. Location detection requires your permission. No account is required.

TIMEZONE GUIDANCE

Understanding daylight saving time

A region’s GMT offset may change during the year. Choose an IANA timezone such as America/New_York to apply the rules for a specific date, rather than assuming a fixed offset.

Eastern Daylight Time · GMT−04:00

EDT is the daylight saving offset used by U.S. Eastern locations that observe seasonal clock changes.

Eastern Standard Time · GMT−05:00

EST is the standard offset for those locations. A local time can be skipped when clocks advance or occur twice when clocks move back.

ZoneSync rejects skipped local times and explains when it selects the earlier occurrence of a repeated time. Read NIST’s daylight saving guidance.

Help and common questions

Quick guidance for using ZoneSync and resolving common issues.

How do I compare timezones?

Open Time zone list, choose a base location on the map, and add one to five different timezones. Select a date, then click the scale or drag its pointer to compare the same instant across all rows.

Are my saved clocks available on other devices?

Saved clocks are stored in this browser and are not synced across devices. Use the same browser and site address to return to them. Clearing site data removes saved clocks and appearance preferences.

What should I do if the map does not load?

Check your internet connection and refresh the page. External map services may be unavailable or blocked by your network. You can also use city search and the timezone dropdowns. For local use, start the application with its launcher.

Why is a local time rejected or repeated?

Daylight saving changes can skip or repeat local times. Choose a time outside a skipped interval. For repeated times, the calculator uses the earlier occurrence and displays an explanation.
